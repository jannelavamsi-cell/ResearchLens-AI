## 2. Comparative Synthesis Narrative

### 1. Methodological Approaches & Algorithmic Trade-offs

The compared papers share a common objective: enhancing Question-Answering (QA) over complex, structured, or multi-page documents by addressing the limitations of traditional, plain-text Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) [PDFTriage: Question Answering over Long, Structured Documents, p. 1; PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering, p. 1; NeuSym-RAG: Hybrid Neural Symbolic Retrieval with Multiview Structuring for PDF Question Answering, p. 1; Doc-React: Multi-page Heterogeneous Document Question-answering, p. 1; MHier-RAG: Multi-Modal RAG for Visual-Rich Document Question-Answering via Hierarchical and Multi-Granularity Reasoning, p. 1]. 

#### Similarities in Objectives and Methodologies
- **Exploiting Document Structure:** Rather than treating documents as unformatted plain text, multiple approaches leverage document layouts (such as headers, footers, sections, pages, and tables) to improve context retrieval [PDFTriage, p. 1; PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering, p. 3; NeuSym-RAG, p. 1].
- **Iterative and Multi-Stage Retrieval:** Several frameworks utilize multi-turn interactions or iterative refinement loops where an LLM acts as an agent or judge to gather sufficient context progressively [PDFTriage, p. 5; NeuSym-RAG, p. 1; Doc-React, p. 1].
- **Multimodal Integration:** Frameworks target documents containing rich visual elements—including images, vector diagrams, graphs, and tables—by combining textual and visual data within the RAG process [PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering, p. 1; Doc-React, p. 1; MHier-RAG, p. 1].

#### Differences and Algorithmic Trade-offs
- **PDFTriage** relies on GPT-3.5 and text-embedding-ada-002 to perform multi-stage querying, allowing models to retrieve context dynamically based on either document structure or content [PDFTriage, p. 1, 5]. 
- **PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering** introduces the PIER-QA system, which preprocesses PDFs via DBSCAN clustering and Marker, embeds 1000-character chunks using GTE-large, indexes via ElasticSearch and RAPTOR, and applies a RAG-aware LoRA-finetuned Llama3-70B model with explicit image/table recovery capabilities [PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering, p. 3]. Its trade-off is a rigid pipeline that always performs retrieval per query, causing latency overhead [PDF Retrieval Augmented Question Answering, p. 8].
- **NeuSym-RAG** adopts a hybrid neural-symbolic retrieval framework. It utilizes multi-view chunking and schema-based parsing to organize semi-structured PDF content into both relational databases and vectorstores, allowing LLM agents to iteratively gather context [NeuSym-RAG, p. 1]. While powerful, its parsing and encoding pipeline is relatively slow due to heavy OCR demands [NeuSym-RAG, p. 9].
- **Doc-React** frames multi-page heterogeneous document QA as an adaptive iterative process using InfoNCE-guided retrieval to approximate mutual information, dynamic sub-query generation, and entropy-aware selection with an LLM acting as both judge and generator [Doc-React, p. 1].
- **MHier-RAG** implements a hierarchical indexing strategy consisting of flattened in-page chunks (for in-page multi-modal associations) and topological cross-page chunks (for long-distance dependencies), combined with page-level parent page retrieval, LLM re-ranking, and document-level summary retrieval [MHier-RAG, p. 1].
